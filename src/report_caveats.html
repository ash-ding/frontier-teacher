A pilot, not a converged comparison. 20 optimisation steps is where standard GRPO recipes use hundreds. These curves answer whether movement happens and whether it differs by band. Several are still rising at the last point.

  • The middle band is confounded. Holding rollouts per step fixed means group size also fixes how many distinct problems a step covers (512/G): the middle bands saw 64 problems per step against the extremes' 16, and four times the data over a run. A matched-group-size re-run is the dashed line; where it is absent, no middle-band comparison should be read as difficulty alone.
  • The thinking rows carry a confound that cannot be removed. Qwen3-4B in thinking mode has only 851 problems in the whole 12k pool with 0 < pass@1 < 1, so its hard band is 100 problems seen 3.2 times while its easy band is 400 seen 0.8 times. Its MATH-500 row is also at ceiling — 95.9% before training.
  • One cell is incomplete. The thinking hard band ran out of GPU memory at step 19 of 20 and has no 10,240-rollout endpoint; its last point is marked with a ring and is not comparable with the other endpoints.
  • Llama's training pool excludes contaminated problems. It scores 61.0% on problems from the original MATH train split against 40.3% on test-derived ones, so its bands draw only from the 4,498 clean problems.
